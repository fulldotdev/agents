# LinkedIn

## Reposts in the user's voice

- Add the user's perspective. Start with what he believes, then describe his role and way of working in the first person.
- For development work, describe how he keeps work moving: get something working quickly, then build, test, learn, and adjust.
- Use the client and project as a short example. Focus on his contribution and what he enjoys about the work.

## Carousels

- Portrait slides are 1080 × 1350 px unless the request or current platform guidance says otherwise.
