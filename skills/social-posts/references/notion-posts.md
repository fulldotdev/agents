# Social posts in Notion Tasks

Use the existing Task for each agreed post. Read its properties and full Timeline before editing. Newer decisions recorded from their source replace earlier draft or scheduling instructions.

## Content

Keep the editable post above `## Timeline`, in this order:

1. `Caption`: the main caption.
2. `Platform captions`: channel variants when needed.
3. `Media`: ordered native images or videos, with source context and captions.
4. `Publication`: confirmed publication facts or open details, when needed.

Keep supporting files, source URLs, and verified published links in the Task's `Resources`, with useful names such as `LinkedIn post`. Do not duplicate the caption or add separate Post relations or publishing-status fields.

Use the Task's normal Status for progress, `Date` for an agreed publication date, and Sprint for routine planning. A finished draft is not a published post. Record whether you checked the public post or the user reported it published. Never invent a public URL. A draft in a canceled Task is not a commitment.

Use native uploaded media, keep its order and quality, and check it after a move or upload. Keep feedback and approvals in the append-only Timeline. Change the editable post only within the request.

Publish or schedule only with the user's approval for that post, then check the result and save the public URL in Resources.
