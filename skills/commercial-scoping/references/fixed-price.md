# Fixed-price work

For a substantial fixed-price scope, group outcomes into categories with one customer price per category. List smaller deliverables below each category without separate prices. Give work that is independently optional its own price. For a small addition, one amount and a clear included scope may be enough.

Include normal implementation, coordination, feedback, testing, and release in the deliverable. Put any risk allowance into the affected category instead of selling a generic contingency or feedback rounds. Explain assumptions that could change the price. Label real unresolved dependencies `❓ Open punt` when useful.

Keep estimated effort, the rate, and detailed calculations internal. Customer scopes use euros unless Sil requests hours. If the customer document and internal calculation are separate, verify that their category totals still match after revisions. End a full customer scope at its total table unless Sil requests notes below it.

Use v1/v2 or separately priced options only when there is a real delivery choice. Do not add a smaller variant to the agreed scope or defer the larger one without support in the agreement.

For website scopes, use these terms consistently:

- **layout**: a complete page or route composition;
- **block**: a pagebuilder or full-width content section;
- **component**: a smaller reusable interface or functional element.

Check existing implementation when it affects the estimate. Price the remaining change rather than rebuilding an already usable foundation in the calculation.
