# Recurring services

Start from the current agreement: what is included, for whom, at what price and billing frequency. Distinguish a fixed service fee from reserved hours or usage-based billing; do not invent rollover, overage, cancellation or availability terms.

Separate one-off setup from ongoing service.

For several brands or companies, check which costs are shared and charge them once. Prefer a separate shared document when that fits the actual payer and agreement. Label genuine alternatives `Optie 1`, `Optie 2`, etc., without making required work look optional.

When changing a package, explain what changes, the new recurring amount and the agreed effective date. Preserve prior rates and terms until a change is approved. Use an extra quote only for work outside the current agreement.
