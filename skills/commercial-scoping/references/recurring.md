# Recurring services

Start from the current agreement: what is included, for whom, at what price, and how often it is billed. Distinguish a fixed service fee from reserved hours or usage-based billing. Do not invent rollover, overage, cancellation, or availability terms.

Separate one-off setup from ongoing service.

For several brands or companies, identify shared costs and charge them once. Use a separate shared document when that fits the payer and agreement. Label real alternatives `Optie 1`, `Optie 2`, and so on. Do not make required work look optional.

When changing a package, explain what changes, the new recurring amount, and the agreed start date. Keep the old rates and terms until the change is approved. Use an extra quote only for work outside the current agreement.
