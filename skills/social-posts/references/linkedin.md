# LinkedIn

Use this reference for LinkedIn captions and carousels.

## Copy

- Write as a person sharing real work, not as a brand performing thought leadership.
- Keep paragraphs short and the progression easy to scan.
- Build around one observation. One or two concrete cases are usually enough for an explanatory post.
- Name clients and tag them naturally when the handle and permission are known.
- Avoid generic engagement questions, forced vulnerability, DM prompts, and requests to answer in the comments.
- A technical post should explain what changed for the person using or operating the software.

## Carousels

- Use 1080 × 1350 px for portrait slides unless the request or current platform guidance requires another size.
- Make the cover state the post's point or promise.
- Give each following slide one distinct example, step, or piece of proof.
- Prefer full-color, readable source material. Keep important screenshots complete and use the same aspect ratio across comparable slides.
- Remove repeated headers, page furniture, borders, and card-in-card layouts when they only consume space.
- Do not add a recap or closing slide unless it adds a useful conclusion or action that is absent elsewhere.

The caption and carousel should work together. Do not copy the full caption onto the slides.
