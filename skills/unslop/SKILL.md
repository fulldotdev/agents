# Unslop

Use as a light final edit. Preserve the meaning, necessary uncertainty, and requested tone. For customer drafts, let `customer-communication` guide the voice and conversation context.

- Replace filler, inflated claims, vague attribution, and abstract jargon with what happened, what it does, or what the reader needs to decide.
- Remove repeated conclusions, empty availability lines, unnecessary praise, and formatting that repeats the same point. Keep a real question, useful acknowledgment, or natural closing.
- Prefer familiar words and sentences that can be understood on the first reading. Keep technical terms when they are more precise for the reader.
- Use structure when it helps. Do not force a fixed number of points, varied sentence lengths, opinions, humor, emojis, or deliberate mess to sound human.
- Never use em dashes. Parentheses, colons, and other punctuation are fine when they help the sentence.

There is no word blacklist. Do not strip useful details, source attribution, or qualifications to make prose shorter. The check is whether the reader understands the intended point, not whether the text passes an AI-style detector.
