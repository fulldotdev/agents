# X

Keep the post focused enough to understand without opening a thread. Use short paragraphs and one useful link. Skip hashtags, decorative emoji, generic questions, and launch hype unless the user asks for them.

For release posts, name the version, what changed, and one concrete measure of scope. Summarize the result instead of pasting a changelog.

## Launch media

- Inspect the live brand website or current brand source before designing the asset. Do not infer the brand from generic product-launch conventions.
- Reuse the brand's typography, spacing, colors, and familiar visual elements. Prefer a card focused on text or a real product screenshot over a decorative collage of interfaces.
- Give a single launch image one headline, at most one supporting line, and one visual motif. The image may identify the release, but it should not repeat the full caption.
- Check readability at feed size. Remove fake interface labels, browser chrome, tiny copy, grids, glow, gradients, and extra decoration unless they belong to the brand.
- For a single landscape asset, a wide 16:9 composition is a practical default. Keep essential copy away from the outer crop area.
- For media made on a remote machine, provide preview and download links that Sil can open in a browser. A temporary server accessible through the private Tailscale network is suitable for review.
