# Fixed-price work

For a substantial fixed-price scope, group deliverable outcomes into categories with one customer price per category. List smaller deliverables underneath without individual prices; give independently optional work its own price. For a small addition, one amount and its included scope can suffice.

Include normal implementation, coordination, feedback, testing and release in the deliverable. Keep any risk allowance in the affected price, rather than selling generic contingency or feedback rounds. Explain assumptions that could change the price, and label genuine unresolved dependencies `❓ Open punt` when useful.

Keep effort, the rate used and detailed calculations internal. Customer scopes use euros unless Sil requests hours. When there is a separate customer document and internal calculation, verify that their category totals match after revisions. End a full customer scope at its total table unless Sil requests notes below it.

Use v1/v2 or separately priced options only when there is an actual delivery choice. Do not automatically add a small variant to the agreed scope or defer the larger one without a basis in the agreement.

For website scopes, use these terms consistently:

- **layout**: a complete page or route composition;
- **block**: a pagebuilder or full-width content section;
- **component**: a smaller reusable interface or functional element.

Check existing implementation when it affects the estimate. Price the remaining change rather than rebuilding an already usable foundation in the calculation.
